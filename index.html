Why TBC

Reasons to ride with us

🎯

Ride with a purpose

Every ride has a goal – fitness, charity, a brevet medal or simply a better Sunday.

🗺️

Discover hidden gems

Quiet lanes, hill roads and sunrise spots across Kerala that most people never see.

🦺

Safety first, always

Safety briefings, ride marshals, identification cards and accident insurance support.

⛰️

Unmatched adventure

200 km to 1200 km Audax brevets, challenges and group tours for every ambition.

📈

Build riding skills

Learn pacing, group riding, night riding and bike maintenance from experienced members.

🤝

A real community

Riders from commuters to randonneurs – you will always find someone at your pace.

Our journey

How TBC rolled out

  1. Beginning of the ride

    A handful of friends start riding together in Trivandrum.

  2. The ride to unity

    Weekly group rides grow into a registered club (2016).

  3. Achievements

    Members complete brevets, Super Randonneur series and 25,000 km years.

  4. Riding challenges

    Cycling league, Green Commute and Hiranraj Challenges bring everyone out.

  5. Building a legacy

    Mentoring new riders and promoting safe, green mobility.
